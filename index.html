📚 Literacy Rate: A Slow but Steady Climb

The literacy rate is a foundational pillar of national development. Over the past two decades, Pakistan has seen a gradual increase, indicating consistent efforts in education, though the pace of improvement highlights ongoing challenges.

💧 Health & Sanitation Nexus

As access to clean water improves, infant mortality decreases — showcasing the powerful impact of public health infrastructure.

🏫 Primary School Enrollment

92% enrolled — significant progress, yet the remaining 8% represents millions of children still out of school.

📈 Economic Growth vs. Unemployment

While GDP per capita has expanded, unemployment has remained stubbornly stable — suggesting that economic growth hasn't fully translated into proportional job creation.

Takeaways

Critical Insights

The most significant findings from two decades of development data.

📚

Education Progress

Literacy has risen from ~50% to ~65% over 20 years. While encouraging, this pace means universal literacy remains decades away without accelerated intervention.

💧

Water-Health Correlation

Every 5% increase in clean water access correlates with approximately 7-point reduction in infant deaths per 1,000 births — a powerful policy lever.

💼

Jobless Growth

GDP per capita nearly doubled, yet unemployment stayed between 4-10%. This "jobless growth" pattern signals structural economic issues.
